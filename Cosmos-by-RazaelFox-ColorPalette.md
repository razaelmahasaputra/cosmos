## Brand Direction

Cosmos by RazaelFox uses a minimal, futuristic, and scalable visual identity.
The palette is optimized for:

- WhatsApp bots
- Discord bots
- Dashboards
- Developer tools
- AI assistants
- Mobile-first interfaces

The design emphasizes strong contrast, readability, and recognition at both small and large sizes.
